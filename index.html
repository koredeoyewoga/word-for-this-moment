The music is played live by the app in a soft gospel style, so there are no adverts and it never sounds exactly the same twice. It goes quieter by itself whenever a verse or prayer is read aloud. "My own music" lets you pick a gospel track saved on your phone. On iPhone, if you hear nothing, turn up the volume.

For a richer voice on iPhone: open Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → Voices → English, download an Enhanced voice (Daniel, Arthur or Serena sound warm), then close and reopen this app and choose it here. On Android or a computer, Chrome's “Google UK English” voices and Edge's “Natural” voices sound best.
